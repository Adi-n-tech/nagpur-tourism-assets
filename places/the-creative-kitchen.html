The Creative Kitchen

The Creative Kitchen is a fine dining restaurant located inside the Radisson Blu, one of the top five star hotels in Nagpur. Head to this stylish restaurant if you want to gorge on an extensive buffet spread, considered one of the best ones in the whole city. Their menu includes various dishes from North Indian and South Indian to Italian and Continental cuisines. You can enjoy complimentary mocktails along with live music on a fun evening with family or friends.
