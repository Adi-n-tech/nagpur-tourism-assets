Dalchini Curries-n-More

A great option if you want to indulge in Maharashtrian style non-vegetarian delights, Dalchini is a budget-friendly option for meat lovers. It is a modern restaurant catering to those who love classy Kolhapuri non-veg cuisine and is one of the reasonable places where you can enjoy egg, chicken, mutton, and vegetarian thalis. Great hospitality and pocket-friendly menu make Dalchini a great option worth a try while you are in the city.

⭐ Traveler Rating 4.0 / 5.0
🚗 Distance from City 2.2 km
🎟️ Admission Fee Free
