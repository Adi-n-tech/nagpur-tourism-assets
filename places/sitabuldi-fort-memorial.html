Sitabuldi Fort & Memorial

Historic hill fortress constructed by Mudhoji II Bhonsle of Nagpur. Site of the fierce 1817 Battle of Sitabuldi between the Marathas and the British East India Company. Offers stunning panoramic 360-degree views of Nagpur city.

⭐ Traveler Rating 4.7 / 5.0
🚗 Distance from City 1.0 km
🎟️ Admission Fee Free (Open on National Holidays & Selected Weekends)

Visiting Hours & Location

Weekly Hours: 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM (Special Days)

Location: Sitabuldi Hill, Sitabuldi, Nagpur
