Haldiram's Sweets & Namkeen

Haldiram’s, the name itself is enough! The brand value of this restaurant is so high that it has garnered a huge fan following all over India with its chain of restaurants, and Nagpur is no different . No wonder, regulars swear by the good quality of food served here and the place is almost full during the morning and evening hours due to the quality and reasonable prices. Go to Haldiram Nagpur to have mouth-watering food and you will not be disappointed, for certain. Though the brand has many outlets in the city, the one in Sadar is the most popular one.

🚗 Distance from City 4.2 km
🎟️ Admission Fee Free
