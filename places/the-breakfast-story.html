The Breakfast Story

The Breakfast Story, as the name suggests, serves all-day breakfasts which are absolutely delectable! Once you step inside, you can see that great emphasis has been placed on the interiors and the looks of the restaurant, featuring second-hand goods recycled to lend a quirky and funky charm to the whole place. You will never have the same food each time you visit here, as the restaurant menu changes daily to bring to you innovative and new recipes. A great place to curl up and read your favorite book or just hang out with your friends, the Breakfast Story is a great option!

⭐ Traveler Rating 4.3 / 5.0
🚗 Distance from City 8.2 km
🎟️ Admission Fee Free

Visiting Hours & Location

Weekly Hours: 8 AM to 7 PM

Location: Sagar Apartment, 271 Hingna Road, Opp. Transmitting Station, Jaitala, Nagpur
