Pench Tiger Reserve & National Park

World-famous tiger reserve nestled in the Satpura hills, inspiring Rudyard Kipling's 'The Jungle Book'. Home to Royal Bengal Tigers, leopards, Indian bison (gaur), deer, and over 285 species of birds with multiple core safari gates like Sillari, Touria, and Khursapar.

⭐ Traveler Rating 4.9 / 5.0
🚗 Distance from City 78.0 km
🎟️ Admission Fee Rs. 2500 - 4500/- (Gypsy Safari Package)

Visiting Hours & Location

Weekly Hours: 6:00 AM to 10:30 AM, 2:30 PM to 6:00 PM

Location: Sillari Gate / Paoni, Nagpur-Jabalpur Highway (NH 44)
