Umred Karhandla Wildlife Sanctuary

A premier wildlife haven near Nagpur famous for frequent tiger sightings, connecting Pench, Tadoba, and Nagzira corridors. Renowned for its tiger lineage (Jai & Srinivas) and rich birdlife.

⭐ Traveler Rating 4.7 / 5.0
🚗 Distance from City 58.0 km
🎟️ Admission Fee Rs. 2000 - 3500/- (Per Gypsy Safari)

Visiting Hours & Location

Weekly Hours: Closed

Location: Umred, Kuhi & Pauni Gates, Nagpur District
