Dragon Palace Lotus Buddhist Temple

Renowned Buddhist temple in Kamptee, symbolized as the 'Lotus Temple of Nagpur'. Built in 1999 as a mark of Indo-Japanese friendship, housing a revered sandalwood statue of Lord Buddha amidst immaculate Japanese Zen gardens.

⭐ Traveler Rating 4.7 / 5.0
🚗 Distance from City 19.0 km
🎟️ Admission Fee Free

Visiting Hours & Location

Weekly Hours: 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM

Location: Gautam Nagar, Kamptee, Nagpur
