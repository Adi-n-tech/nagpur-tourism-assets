Haldiram's

Haldiram's is the place for desserts and fast food. There are many branches of haldiram's in Nagpur. This is one of the branch. Ambience is top class. Food quality is very good but slightly on a higher price side. The only problem is the food gets exhausted in the evening. They run out of food everyday and still don't anything for it. Food presentation is that good.

⭐ Traveler Rating 4.3 / 5.0
🚗 Distance from City 7.1 km
🎟️ Admission Fee Free

Visiting Hours & Location

Weekly Hours: 9 AM to 10 PM

Location: Opp Podale Hospital, Trimurtee Nagar, Nagpur
